# Learning Center guides — exact steps (source text for video scripts)

*Copied read-only from the live app on 17 Nov 2026 (`/branch/<id>/tutorials`
and `/hq/tutorials`). Every guide still shows "Video lesson — Video coming soon".
Each video should follow these steps in this order, but use the **on-screen
labels** where the guide wording differs (see the table at the end).*

Format: **Title** — category · read time · "Try it in the app" button.

## Branch Learning Center (22)

### Sales & POS

**Make your first sale** — Sales & POS · 3 min · *Open Point of Sale*
1. Open Point of Sale and search for a medicine or scan its barcode.
2. Choose the available batch and quantity, then review the cart and total.
3. Select the payment method and complete the sale.
4. Review the receipt before printing or sharing it.
Related: View sales reports · Open patients

**Review a refund or void** — Sales & POS · 4 min · *Open Point of Sale*
1. Find the original transaction and confirm its receipt and items.
2. Choose the refund or void action available for your role and the sale state.
3. Check the quantities and reason before confirming the action.
4. Review the resulting receipt and stock movement.

**Close and review a till** — Sales & POS · 4 min · *Open Cash-up*
1. Open Cash-up and select the shift to review.
2. Compare expected payment totals with the physical cash and other payment records.
3. Record any difference with a clear explanation.
4. Submit or certify the close according to your role.

### Inventory

**Check stock and expiry** — Inventory · 3 min · *Open Inventory*
1. Open Inventory to see products and the stock available in this branch.
2. Search for a product, then inspect its batches, quantities, and expiry dates.
3. Use stock alerts to prioritise items that need replenishment.
4. Run a stocktake when you need to compare shelf counts with the system.
Related: Open stocktakes · View stock value

**Import and receive stock** — Inventory · 5 min · *Open Inventory*
1. Open Inventory, then Imports, and choose the import appropriate to your file.
2. Check the required columns and review the preview before applying it.
3. Resolve any rejected rows and import the valid records.
4. Return to Inventory to confirm quantities, batches, and expiry dates.

**Count stock on the shelf** — Inventory · 5 min · *Open Inventory*
1. Open Stocktakes and start a count for the stock you are checking.
2. Enter the physical quantities from the shelf, batch by batch.
3. Review variances before submitting the count.
4. Follow the approval step when your pharmacy requires one.

**Move stock between branches** — Inventory · 5 min · *Open Transfers*
1. Open Transfers and choose the source and destination branches.
2. Select the products and quantities to move.
3. Review the transfer before sending or approving it.
4. Confirm receipt at the destination so stock balances update.

### Patients & insurance

**Serve an insured patient** — Patients & insurance · 4 min · *Open Insurance*
1. Open Insurance and confirm that the provider and current tariff are available.
2. At Point of Sale, choose insurance at checkout and enter the patient details shown on the card.
3. Review the patient and insurer portions before completing payment.
4. For an RSSB claim, open Insurance and download its claim form from the claim row.
Related: Open Point of Sale · Open patients

**Find and update a patient** — Patients & insurance · 3 min · *Open Patients*
1. Open Patients and search by the information you have.
2. Open the matching record and check the identifying details before editing.
3. Update permitted contact or insurance information.
4. Save and confirm the change appears on the patient record.

**Download an RSSB claim form** — Patients & insurance · 3 min · *Open Insurance*
1. Open Insurance and find the RSSB claim by patient or receipt.
2. Review the form details and update confirmed information where permitted.
3. Choose RSSB form PDF on the claim row.
4. Check the downloaded form before printing or submitting it through your normal process.

### Reports & compliance

**Review sales reports** — Reports & compliance · 3 min · *Open Reports*
1. Open Reports and choose the report you need.
2. Set the date range and branch scope before reading the figures.
3. Open the detail rows to check the transactions behind a total.
4. Export the report when you need a copy outside the app.
Related: Sales report · Fiscal reports

**Review VAT and fiscal records** — Reports & compliance · 4 min · *Open Fiscal Reports*
1. Open Fiscal Reports and select the period to review.
2. Check receipt status and investigate any item that has not been submitted.
3. Open the VAT view when you need tax totals for the same period.
4. Export the relevant report if your role permits it.

### Team & settings

**Set up your pharmacy** — Team & settings · 4 min · *Open Settings*
1. Open Settings and review the pharmacy name and business information.
2. Check branch details and tax settings before using receipts or reports.
3. Invite staff and assign only the access their role needs.
4. Save changes and confirm they appear on the relevant screens.
Related: Open your profile

**Manage staff access** — Team & settings · 4 min · *Open Settings*
1. Open Settings and find staff management.
2. Invite a new user or open an existing staff account.
3. Choose the role and branch access needed for the job.
4. Save and verify the user has the expected access.

### Finance

**Record an expense** — Finance · 4 min · *Open Expenses*
1. Open Expenses and create a new expense.
2. Enter the amount, date, category, and business reason.
3. Add any available reference or supporting document.
4. Review the entry before saving it.

**Track supplier bills** — Finance · 4 min · *Open Supplier Bills*
1. Open Supplier Bills to see recorded invoices and their status.
2. Open a bill to check the supplier, due date, and line amounts.
3. Use Aged Payables to prioritise overdue balances.
4. Record payments using the action available to your role.

**Create a customer invoice** — Finance · 5 min · *Open Invoices*
1. Open Invoices and start a new invoice.
2. Choose the customer and add the products or services to bill.
3. Review quantities, tax, and total before saving.
4. Use Aged Receivables to follow outstanding balances.

**Review money and banking** — Finance · 4 min · *Open Banking*
1. Open Banking and select the account or period you need.
2. Review the recorded transactions and balances.
3. Investigate entries that do not match your statement.
4. Use the available reconciliation action when your role permits it.

### Getting started & help

**Find your way around the dashboard** — Getting started · 4 min · *Open Dashboard*
1. Start with the four summary figures at the top of the dashboard.
2. Open a figure to see the underlying sales or inventory screen.
3. Review the stock and expiry panels for work that needs attention.
4. Use the navigation tabs to move to a task and return to Dashboard for a quick check.

**Manage your profile and security** — Getting started · 3 min · *Open Profile*
1. Open your account menu and choose Profile.
2. Review your name, contact details, and sign-in security settings.
3. Update only the fields that need changing and save them.
4. Check your recent activity if you need to confirm an account action.

**Stay on top of updates** — Getting started · 2 min · *Open Notifications*
1. Open Notifications from the top bar.
2. Read the newest items and open the related screen when available.
3. Return after completing the task to confirm its current status.

**Get help with a problem** — Help · 2 min · *Open Support*
1. Open Support and choose New request.
2. Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened.
3. Include the time, affected screen, and any request ID shown in an error.
4. Return to Support to follow replies and updates.

## Head Office Learning Center (13)

**Watch your branches** — Head office · 3 min · *Open Overview* (paused — hardest)
1. Open Overview to see today's branch activity and connection status.
2. Check the branches marked as needing attention first.
3. Open a branch row to inspect its activity and current issues.
4. Use Exceptions for a focused list of items that need follow-up.

**Check fiscal and stock issues** — Head office · 3 min · *Open Compliance*
1. Open Compliance and review receipts that have not reached RRA.
2. Identify the branch and transaction behind each item.
3. Open the related branch workspace to investigate or correct the issue.
4. Refresh Compliance to confirm the status changed.

**Work through exceptions** — Head office · 4 min · *Open Exceptions*
1. Open Exceptions and review the highest priority items first.
2. Check the branch, event, and time for each item.
3. Open the related branch or report to investigate the cause.
4. Return to Exceptions to confirm the issue is resolved or still needs action.

**Compare branch performance** — Reports & compliance · 3 min · *Open Reports*
1. Open Reports from head office.
2. Choose a report and set the date range and branch scope.
3. Compare the totals, then inspect the underlying rows where needed.
4. Export a copy for review outside the app.

**Review stock across branches** — Inventory · 4 min · *Open Stock*
1. Open Stock from head office to review the organisation's inventory.
2. Find the product you need and compare branch availability.
3. Check low quantities and expiry concerns before deciding on a transfer.
4. Open the relevant branch or Transfers to take action.

**Coordinate branch transfers** — Inventory · 5 min · *Open Transfers*
1. Open Transfers and review requests and stock already in transit.
2. Choose the source, destination, and items for a new movement.
3. Check quantities and approval status before dispatch.
4. Confirm receipt at the destination to complete the movement.

**Review insurance across branches** — Patients & insurance · 4 min · *Open Insurance*
1. Open Insurance from head office and choose the branch or provider you need.
2. Review claim status and the amount due from the insurer.
3. Open a claim to check its patient and sale reference.
4. Follow up on unpaid or rejected amounts through the available actions.

**Find patients across the organisation** — Patients & insurance · 3 min · *Open Patients*
1. Open Patients from head office and search with the details you know.
2. Check the matching identity and branch before opening a record.
3. Review only the information your role is permitted to see.
4. Use the branch workspace for any branch-specific follow-up.

**Manage staff and branch access** — Team & settings · 5 min · *Open Staff*
1. Open Staff and find the person or invitation you need.
2. Review the user's role and branch assignments.
3. Make the access change required for the job.
4. Save and verify the effective access before the user signs in again.

**Set up branches and policies** — Team & settings · 5 min · *Open Governance*
1. Open Governance to review organisation-wide settings.
2. Open Branches to confirm each location's details and status.
3. Review the policy or configuration you intend to change.
4. Save and check the affected branch workspace afterward.

**Review organisation finances** — Finance · 5 min · *Open Accounting*
1. Open Accounting for the organisation-wide view.
2. Choose the period and branch scope you need to review.
3. Use Bills and Expenses for money owed or spent, and Receivables for customer balances.
4. Inspect the underlying entries before exporting or approving a figure.

**Get help with a problem** — Help · 2 min · *Open Support*
1. Open Support and create a new request.
2. Name the affected branch and screen.
3. Include the time, expected result, actual result, and any request ID.
4. Track the reply from Support.

**Follow head office updates** — Getting started · 2 min · *Open Notifications*
1. Open Notifications from the top bar.
2. Read the branch and action linked to each update.
3. Open the related screen and complete the follow-up.

## Guide wording vs on-screen labels (check before scripting)

| Guide says | What the app actually shows (17 Nov 2026) |
|---|---|
| "Imports" | Inventory → **Purchases & imports** (RRA supplier invoices / customs) or toolbar **Import CSV or Excel** (file import) |
| "Stocktakes" | Inventory → **Cycle counts** (+ Count approvals) |
| "Cash-up" | Branch **Accounting → Cash-up** tab; at the till it's **End Shift** |
| "Supplier Bills", "Aged Payables" | Branch **Expenses → Bills**; AP Aging reports under Reports → Payables |
| "Invoices", "Aged Receivables", "Banking" | Not seen as top-level labels — likely under Accounting (*Money accounts*) and Reports → Receivables. **Confirm with MalFour** |
| "Choose the available batch" (first sale) | The POS product list shows stock and expiry badge per product; batch choice wasn't observed without a live sale |
| "Profile" | Account menu, top right |

These mismatches are worth reporting to MalFour too — the written guides
themselves would confuse users in the same way.
