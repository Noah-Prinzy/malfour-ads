# MalFour brand — quick reference

**Source of truth:** the official *MalFour Limited Brand Guidelines* (May 2026),
[PDF](../../assets/brand-identity/MalFour%20Limited%20-%20Brand%20Identity/Brand%20Guidelines/MalFour%20Limited%20Brand%20Guidlines.pdf)
and `.ai` in `assets/brand-identity/`. This page summarises it for video and ad
work. It **supersedes** the earlier web-sampled palette (maroon `#610000`,
cream, Manrope) recorded in older plans — where an older plan says cream or
Manrope, use the values below.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary — MalFour red** | `#BA000C` | Guidelines primary (RGB 186, 0, 12). Section backgrounds, CTAs, accents |
| **Logo red** (slash in the mark) | `#BE1E2D` | As used in the official SVG/PNG logo files — don't recolour the logo to `#BA000C` |
| **Secondary — white** | `#FFFFFF` | Base / backgrounds |
| **Tertiary — charcoal** | `#2A2A2A` | Logo letters, dark backgrounds, body text on light |
| Black | `#000000` | |
| Neutral 01 | `#7B8087` | Secondary text, muted UI |
| Neutral 02 | `#CACBCC` | Dividers, light panels |
| Neutral 03 | light grey (hex left blank in the guidelines; the slides use ≈ `#EDF0F3`) | Panel backgrounds — confirm the exact value with MalFour |

Approved logo backgrounds (p.15): red, charcoal, navy, black, mid-grey, light
grey, white — always with enough contrast.

## Typography

- **Inter** for everything (OFL licence, files in
  `assets/brand-identity/…/Brand Guidelines/Fonts/`).
- Hierarchy: **Header — Inter Bold** · **Sub-header — Inter SemiBold** ·
  **Body — Inter Regular**.

## Logo

- Three forms: **logomark** (the red slash + charcoal "f" in a rounded square),
  **logotype** ("Mal⁄four" wordmark where the slash forms the F), **full logo**
  (mark + wordmark lockup). Main and Alternate versions are supplied as SVG,
  transparent PNG and non-transparent PNG.
- **Minimum size:** logomark 10 mm / 50 px; logotype and full logo 50 mm / 150 px.
- **Clearspace:** one logomark-square on every side.
- **Never:** stretch/skew, recolour single parts, rotate, add drop shadows or
  strokes, or modify any part.
- **Brand pattern:** repeated logomark on red (white + charcoal) or on charcoal
  (red + white) — for backgrounds and accents.

## Tone of voice (p.7)

Clear & trustworthy · Empowering & inclusive · Forward-looking & practical ·
Professional & human ("authoritative yet approachable").

## Photography (p.29–30)

Real people in real East African settings (stock rooms, markets, offices,
training); technology as an enabler of progress; bright, natural, optimistic.
**Avoid** posed, generic, poorly lit, dark/moody or heavily filtered images.

## In-product branding (seen in the live app)

- App name: **MalFour Pharmacy System**; header "MalFour Pharmacy — Head Office"
  / "— *Branch name*".
- The pharmacy's own logo prints on receipts **between the RRA mark and the
  national seal**.
- The product UI is light (white/grey) with charcoal text, red for alerts and
  primary buttons in black — screen recordings sit naturally on a white or
  light-grey brand frame.
