## Who they are

**MalFour Limited** is a software company building **ERP, POS and business
intelligence** tools for businesses **"across Africa"**, with East Africa as the
home market. The flagship product is the **MalFour Pharmacy System**
(integrated ERP + POS for pharmacies) — see
[../product/SYSTEM-STUDY.md](../product/SYSTEM-STUDY.md).

## Purpose, vision, mission, values (Brand Guidelines p.5–6)

- **Purpose:** empower businesses across East Africa with accessible,
  compliant, integrated digital tools that simplify operations, ensure **tax
  compliance**, and drive sustainable growth.
- **Vision:** become the region's most trusted **digital infrastructure
  partner**.
- **Mission:** integrated, **low-cost**, compliant solutions that bridge the
  digital divide and support local enterprises.
- **Core values:** Innovation (research-driven, local needs) · Trust
  (transparency, data protection, reliability) · Accessibility
  (**low-bandwidth**, affordable, inclusive) · Impact (measurable outcomes) ·
  Sustainability.

### Pharmacy System as sold on the site (/uganda/pharmacy)

- Tagline: *"Pharmacy management for teams in Uganda: sales, stock,
  prescriptions and reporting in one system."*
- Features listed: barcode / prescription checkout, **FEFO** expiry-aware stock,
  batch tracking, purchase orders, multi-branch, shift reconciliation,
  role-based access, 2FA, audit trail, cash / **mobile money** / insurance
  payments.
- Tiers *Starter* (independent pharmacies), *Pro* (growing / multi-branch,
  "Most Popular"), *Custom* (large chains). (Prices are shown on the Rwanda
  page — see below.)
- **Referral offer:** 25% off for existing customers who refer a pharmacy.
- Positioning: *"Built for how African pharmacies actually work"* — offline
  capable, mobile money, local and tax compliance; explicitly contrasted with
  "a Western system… localised".
- The homepage is careful about AI: "intelligent" = rule-based automation and
  statistical pattern detection. **No AI claims.**

### Rwanda product page (malfour.com/rwanda/pharmacy) — read 17 Nov 2026

The site has **one product page per country**. The Rwanda page is the one that
matches how the live system is built (RRA EBM, RSSB).

- Why MalFour: Works offline · Secure & role-based · Local compliance ready
  (*"We review Rwanda pharmacy and invoicing requirements with your team before

- **Public pricing** (flat monthly subscription, no setup fees, no contracts;
  billing monthly / quarterly / 6 months / annual; VAT excluded, 18% may apply):

  | Plan | Price | For | Highlights |

  - *Multi-branch* — centralised reporting, per-branch stock visibility.

## Notes for marketing work

- **Market:** the product is built for **Rwanda**, and malfour.com has a
  dedicated Rwanda page with RWF pricing — so the Rwanda campaign brief fits.
  The Uganda page is a parallel country version; Uganda ads would need
  Uganda-specific compliance claims checked first.
- **Pricing is public in Rwanda** (35k / 100k RWF a month), so ads *can* name a
  "from 35,000 RWF a month" price — check with MalFour that it's current.
- **Proof gaps:** the site has no testimonials, case studies or customer logos
  (only PSF membership), and no figures behind "hours saved" or "measurable
  growth". Don't put numbers in ads until sourced.
- **Calls to action** on the site are "Request a demo" (malfour.com/request)
  and "Contact sales" — mirror those words in end cards.
- Health and pharmacy ad categories carry platform policy restrictions on
  targeting and claims — check per platform before paid runs.
- Tone on the site: plain, direct, low-pressure ("No pressure, just a
  conversation"). Matches the brand tone of voice in [../brand/BRAND.md](../brand/BRAND.md).
